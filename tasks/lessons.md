# Lessons

## 2026-10-16: first version was too busy
The owner rejected v1.0 (ticker, percent change and price on a 52×16 panel): "too busy to read".
What he wants on the clock: one idea per page, no secondary numbers, direction carried by
colour and a shape (^ / v chevron), the ticker prominent, a logo for recognition.
Rule for future pixel-clock apps: show the headline value plus one visual cue; put everything
else behind the knob or a setting. Ask about density before building, or build the sparse
version first.

## 2026-10-16: an alert must show the number it is about
The first big-move pause froze the scrolling line, so the logo sat on screen and the
percentage was off to the right. The owner saw it live. Rule: any attention-grabbing state
gets its own composed frame (what, how much, in colour), never a paused version of a
frame designed for motion.

## 2026-10-16: show layouts before building, and let reading time come from a setting
Three corrections in one evening on the same screen: too busy, the alert card transition,
then "every stock should pause" and "effects off by default". The pattern: on this panel
The owner wants calm, uniform frames with enough dwell to read, and decoration opt-in. For
anything visual, render two to four real options on the device and ask before coding the
behaviour around them; the options page took 20 minutes and settled the design in one
reply.
