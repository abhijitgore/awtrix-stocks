Stock page layouts

Four ways to put ticker, a big price and a small percentage on one 52×16 frame, so a big move only adds an effect behind the same frame instead of switching to a card. Every capture below was read back from your clock with today's data.

Panel 52 × 16 LEDs. A logo takes columns 0–15, leaving 34 columns for text.
Fonts small (5 rows) and large (7 rows) are both 4 px per character. matrix-chunky8x6 is 8 rows tall and 6 px per character: the only genuinely bigger price.
Fit beside a logo: ticker 16 px + 2 px gap + "+0.4%" 18 px = 36 px on the small row, so the change gets one decimal. A six-character price in the 8×6 font is 36 px and spills over by 2 px, so A and B show 343.5 instead of 343.46.
OPTION A

Header row, big price

Keep the logo. Ticker and change sit on a small top row; the price gets the tall 8×6 font underneath.

At one pixel per LED

Gains

  • Price is the biggest thing on the panel
  • Change is small but coloured, so it still pops
  • Nothing moves; a big move just adds fireworks or rain behind this same frame

Costs

  • Change shows one decimal (+0.4%) to fit beside a 4-letter ticker
  • Prices of 1000 or more drop decimals (1234 → four digits) to fit beside the logo
  • Five-letter tickers (GOOGL) would collide with the change; they'd need a 2 px squeeze or the change loses its % sign
OPTION B

Big price on top

Same ingredients as A, flipped: the price leads at the top, ticker and change read as a caption below.

At one pixel per LED

Gains

  • Eye lands on the price first, which is what you glance for
  • Caption row reads like a label under a number

Costs

  • Same one-decimal and long-price limits as A
  • Ticker is the smallest thing on screen; with ten stocks you may have to read the logo to know which it is
OPTION C

No logo, full width

Drop the logo and use all 52 columns: two-decimal change on the top row, the full price in the tall font.

At one pixel per LED

Gains

  • Full precision: +0.43% and 343.46
  • Room for five-letter tickers and six-figure prices (BRK-A)
  • Cleanest text, nothing is squeezed

Costs

  • No logo. You liked the logos
  • Pages look alike at a glance; colour and the ticker text do all the work
OPTION D

Logo, coloured price

Keep the logo and the layout of A, but set the price in the 7-row font and colour it by direction.

At one pixel per LED

Gains

  • Full two-decimal price always fits (22 px for six characters)
  • Direction is carried by the price colour itself, visible from across the room
  • Six-figure prices still fit

Costs

  • Price is tall but not as wide as the 8×6 font; less of a hero
  • Change still one decimal beside the ticker
  • Green or red price text can look like an alert even on an ordinary day
